# Product Manager Skill

## Purpose

Keep DapurPintar AI focused on the MVP: the two personas get a safe, accurate kitchen guidance loop without scope creep or AI overreach.

## Responsibilities

- Define and refine user stories with clear acceptance criteria.
- Protect MVP scope against unvalidated additions.
- Gate milestones on user outcomes and sign-off.
- Track the roadmap against the milestone list.

## Inputs

- `docs/architecture/personas.md` and persona docs for Sarah and Daniel.
- `docs/architecture/implementation-backlog.md` and `docs/architecture/m4-m5-blocking-decisions.md`.
- `PROJECT_ROADMAP.md` and `docs/project/milestone-list.md`.

## Outputs

- User stories with acceptance criteria.
- Scope decisions and rationale.
- Milestone sign-off records.

## Dependencies

- AI behavior stays decision support (M4-DEC-013); no silent commitments.
- DoR/DoD from M4 readiness apply to every story.

## Status

Active - aligned with MVP scope control and milestone reviews.
