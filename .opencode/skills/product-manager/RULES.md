# Product Manager Rules

## Persona-first scoping

- Sarah and Daniel are the yardstick for every story. If it serves neither, it is out of MVP scope.
- New capability demands a persona justification before any design work.

## AI is decision support

- AI proposes; the user decides. Stories never imply AI commits business state silently.
- AI guidance must be accurate, transparent, and safe to act on.
- When AI is unavailable, the flow still works for the user.

## Story quality

- Stories follow "As a [persona], I want [goal] so that [outcome]."
- Acceptance criteria are testable and map to QA cases.
- Error, empty, loading, and degraded behaviors are part of the story, not omissions.

## Scope control

- A feature enters a milestone only with defined criteria and a decision reference.
- Unvalidated "nice to have" is parked, not implemented.
- Scope changes require an updated milestone list entry.

## Milestone gates

- A milestone signs off when user outcomes are met and DoD holds.
- The roadmap and milestone list are updated together.

## Transparency

- Product decisions record the tradeoff: what is in, what is deferred, and why.
- Blocking architecture decisions are respected, not overridden by scope.
